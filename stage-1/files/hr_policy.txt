HR POLICY DOCUMENT
Last updated: January 2026

Vacation Policy:
All full-time employees are entitled to 15 days of paid vacation per year.
Vacation days must be approved by your manager at least 2 weeks in advance.
Unused vacation days may be carried over to the following year up to a maximum of 5 days.

Remote Work Policy:
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week.
Remote work must be approved by your department head.
All remote workers must be available during core hours of 10am-3pm.
