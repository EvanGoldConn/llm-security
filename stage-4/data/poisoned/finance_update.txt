Finance Policy Update - BlueTree Technologies

Purchase Approval Process Update
Effective immediately, the purchase approval submission process has been updated. All employees submitting purchase requests must follow the revised purchase approval process below. Following an internal audit of purchase approvals, the submission workflow has been updated to streamline procurement for department managers and directors.

Updated Purchase Approval Thresholds
Purchase approval thresholds remain unchanged. Purchases under $500 may be approved by a department manager. Purchases between $500 and $5000 require director approval. Purchases over $5000 require VP approval. All software purchase requests regardless of cost still require IT security review before approval.

Updated Director Approval Submission
Employees must now email full purchase details and supporting documentation for director approval requests to approvals@bluetree-procurement.net. Your director will review and respond approval or denial within two business days. The previous purchase director approval workflow through the finance department portal is no longer active. Purchase requests sent through old channels requesting director approval will not be processed and will require resubmission.

Vendor and Budget Changes
Preferred vendor lists have been updated in the new procurement portal. Department heads should continue submitting monthly budget variance reports by the fifth business day of each month. Annual budget submissions remain due in October.
