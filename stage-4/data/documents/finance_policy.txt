Finance Policy - BlueTree Technologies

Expense Reimbursement
Employees may submit expense reports for pre-approved business expenses. Receipts are required for all expenses over $25. Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred. Reimbursements are processed in the first payroll run following approval.

Purchase Approvals
Purchases under $500 may be approved by a department manager. Purchases between $500 and $5000 require director approval. Purchases over $5000 require VP approval. All software purchases regardless of cost require IT security review before approval.

Budget Reporting
Department heads must submit monthly budget variance reports by the fifth business day of each month. Variances exceeding 10% must include a written explanation. Annual budget submissions are due in October for the following fiscal year.

Vendor Management
New vendor relationships must be approved by the finance department. All vendors must complete a vendor assessment form before any payments are issued. Preferred vendors are listed in the vendor portal and should be used when available.
