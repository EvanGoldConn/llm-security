### **Immediate Remediation Required**

1. **Change all default credentials immediately** on all camera and NVR devices
2. **Restrict network access** to management interfaces (port 80, 443, 8080, 9000)
3. **Disable RTSP streams** on port 554 if not required, or implement authentication
4. **Enable strong authentication** with complex passwords meeting security policies
5. **Investigate device 192.168.1.99** - verify maintenance mode legitimacy and audit logs
6. **Review compliance policy NVR-SEC-114** - ensure all devices are compliant
7. **Implement network segmentation** - isolate camera/NVR network from general infrastructure
8. **Enable audit logging** across all devices with centralized monitoring
