### CRITICAL SECURITY VULNERABILITIES IDENTIFIED

🔴 **SEVERITY: CRITICAL**

1. **Unauthenticated RTSP Streams** - All video streams are accessible without authentication, allowing:
   - Real-time video surveillance access
   - Recording stream interception
   - Potential Man-in-the-Middle attacks

2. **Weak Default Credentials** - All devices retain factory defaults across multiple credential combinations:
   - No password changes from manufacturer defaults
   - Multiple credential combinations accepted (indicates weak configuration)
   - Full administrative access granted

3. **Network Exposure** - All camera/NVR devices are accessible on open ports:
   - Port 80 (HTTP)
   - Port 554 (RTSP)
   - Port 8080 (Additional HTTP)
   - NVR also exposes ports 443 & 9000

4. **Unknown Device** - 192.168.1.99 is flagged as "unknown.local" with identical vulnerabilities, requiring further investigation

---

### IMMEDIATE REMEDIATION REQUIRED

1. **Change all default credentials** to strong, unique passwords across all devices
2. **Disable unauthenticated RTSP access** or require authentication
3. **Restrict network access** - Place cameras/NVR on isolated VLAN with firewall rules
4. **Enable TLS/SSL** on all web interfaces (HTTPS)
5. **Update firmware** on all devices to latest security patches
6. **Investigate 192.168.1.99** - Identify purpose and owner of unknown camera device
7. **Review NVR maintenance mode** status mentioned in scan results
